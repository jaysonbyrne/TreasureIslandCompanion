# Treasure Island Companion — Asset Plan

Every in-world paper of Stevenson's novel reset as a period document, interleaved
with painted plates. Target density mirrors the Dracula companion: roughly one
document or plate every few pages.

**Plate style (all plates):** painterly maritime illustration in the manner of
N.C. Wyeth's 1911 *Treasure Island* plates — dramatic chiaroscuro, muted
sea-greys, ochre and tar-black palette, 18th-century dress, visible brushwork,
no text or lettering in the image.

**Document style:** 18th-century English paper — laid stock, iron-gall ink,
period hands and printed forms; the map, the black spots, the ship's articles
and the treasure account are the hero props.

## Front matter

- DOC: Dedication "To S.L.O." — engraved dedication page.
- DOC: "To the Hesitating Purchaser" — Stevenson's verse set as a decorated frontispiece page.
- DOC: **Flint's treasure map** — the hero prop; aged chart with the three red crosses, "Bulk of treasure here," Spy-glass Hill, the stockade, compass rose, latitude/longitude notes in Flint's hand.
- PLATE: Frontispiece flat-lay — Billy Bones's sea-chest open: oilskin packet, doubloons, the map's edge, a pistol, tobacco.

## PART ONE — The Old Buccaneer

**Ch 1 — The Old Sea-dog at the Admiral Benbow**
- DOC: The Admiral Benbow's reckoning — Billy Bones's unpaid lodging account, chalked and inked.
- PLATE: Billy Bones arriving at the cove, sea-chest on a hand-barrow behind him, sabre-cut livid on his cheek.

**Ch 2 — Black Dog Appears and Disappears**
- DOC: Handbill — "Wanted: a seafaring man with two fingers missing," Bristol reward notice.
- PLATE: Black Dog and Billy Bones grappling in the inn parlour, cutlasses half-drawn.

**Ch 3 — The Black Spot**
- DOC: **The black spot** — hero prop; a page torn from a Bible, blackened circle, "You have till ten to-night."
- PLATE: Blind Pew tapping up the moonlit lane toward the inn, green shade over his eyes.

**Ch 4 — The Sea-chest**
- DOC: Inventory of Billy Bones's sea-chest, in Jim's hand — quadrant, doubloons, the oilskin packet.
- PLATE: Jim and his mother fleeing the inn with the packet as Pew's whistle sounds.

**Ch 5 — The Last of the Blind Man**
- DOC: Supervisor Dance's excise report — the night ride, Pew trampled, the lugger in Kitt's Hole.
- PLATE: The revenue horses thundering down on Blind Pew in the moonlight.

**Ch 6 — The Captain's Papers**
- DOC: Billy Bones's account book — Flint's treasure tallies, "Offe Caraccas," the sums in faded ink.
- PLATE: Jim laying the oilskin packet before Squire Trelawney and Dr. Livesey.

## PART TWO — The Sea-cook

**Ch 7 — I Go to Bristol**
- DOC: **Squire Trelawney's letter from Bristol** — hero prop; the long letter: the *Hispaniola*, Long John Silver, the crew, "I have been to sea myself."
- PLATE: Jim setting out on the road to Bristol, the letter in his pocket.

**Ch 8 — At the Sign of the Spy-glass**
- DOC: The Spy-glass tavern sign — painted board, "Admiral Benbow" crossed out, parrot perched.
- PLATE: Long John Silver in the tavern doorway, crutch under his arm, parrot on his shoulder, smiling.

**Ch 9 — Powder and Arms**
- DOC: **The Hispaniola's articles** — hero prop; ship's articles signed with crosses and scrawled names.
- DOC: Muster roll of the *Hispaniola* — officers, crew, and the men Silver picked.
- PLATE: The schooner fitting out in Bristol harbour, powder going aboard.

**Ch 10 — The Voyage**
- DOC: Captain Smollett's sailing orders, sealed — course, discipline, "no officer to be contradicted."
- PLATE: The *Hispaniola* under full sail on a blue sea, the voyage begun.

**Ch 11 — What I Heard in the Apple-Barrel**
- DOC: A page from Jim's sea journal — latitude, weather, "the cook's party."
- PLATE: Jim crouched in the apple barrel, Silver's face looming above the rim.

**Ch 12 — Council of War**
- DOC: Smollett's written precautions — arms served out, watches doubled, signed by all three gentlemen.
- PLATE: The cabin council — captain, squire, doctor and Jim around the chart.

**Ch 31 — The Treasure-hunt: Flint's Pointer**
