# Characters of Treasure Island

Every named figure in Stevenson's text, grouped by their place in the story.
(Spellings follow the Project Gutenberg text, ebook #120.)

## The Admiral Benbow

- **Jim Hawkins** — Narrator; innkeeper's son, then cabin-boy of the *Hispaniola*.
- **Mrs. Hawkins** — Jim's mother; keeps the Admiral Benbow inn after her husband's death.
- **Mr. Hawkins** — Jim's father; innkeeper, dies early of illness.
- **Billy Bones ("the Captain")** — Old buccaneer lodging at the inn; dies of a stroke after receiving the black spot.
- **Black Dog** — Pirate with two fingers missing; old shipmate of Billy Bones.
- **Blind Pew** — Blind beggar and pirate; delivers the black spot, then is trampled to death by revenue officers' horses.
- **Supervisor Dance** — Excise officer whose timely ride saves Jim and his mother from Pew's crew.

## The gentlemen of the expedition

- **Squire John Trelawney** — Bristol squire; funds the voyage, loose-tongued but brave.
- **Dr. David Livesey** — Country doctor and magistrate; the expedition's cool head and moral center.
- **Mr. Blandly** — Trelawney's Bristol agent; finds and fits out the *Hispaniola*.

## The Hispaniola's honest company

- **Captain Alexander Smollett** — Master of the *Hispaniola*; strict, suspicious, and right about everything.
- **Mr. Arrow** — First mate; a drunk who vanishes overboard on the outward voyage.
- **Tom Redruth** — The squire's gamekeeper; killed in the first fight at the stockade.
- **John Hunter** — One of the squire's two servants; dies of wounds after the stockade attack.
- **Richard Joyce** — The squire's other servant; shot through the head in the attack.
- **Abraham Gray** — Ship's carpenter; wavers, then deserts the mutineers and joins the faithful.

## Long John Silver's mutineers

- **Long John Silver** — Sea-cook of the *Hispaniola*, once Flint's quartermaster; one leg, one parrot, and a genius for survival.
- **Israel Hands** — Coxswain; Flint's old gunner; murdered by Jim aboard the *Hispaniola*.
- **George Merry** — Mutineer; ringleader of the malcontents against Silver.
- **Tom Morgan** — Mutineer; one of Flint's old crew.
- **Dick Johnson ("Dick")** — The youngest mutineer; carries the black spot to Silver.
- **Job Anderson** — Boatswain, then acting mate after Arrow's loss; mutineer.
- **O'Brien** — Irish mutineer; killed by Israel Hands and left as a corpse aboard ship.
- **Jack** — Minor mutineer, named once during the powder-shifting.

## The island

- **Ben Gunn** — Marooned three years by Flint's crew; half-mad, goatskin-clad, and cheese-obsessed; holds the real secret of the treasure.
- **Allardyce** — Dead pirate of Flint's crew; his skeleton, laid out by compass, marks Flint's pointer.
- **Captain J. Flint** — The legendary dead pirate captain; never appears alive, but his map, his treasure, and his terror drive everything.

## Animals and appendages of note

- **Captain Flint (the parrot)** — Silver's green parrot, screaming "Pieces of eight!"; named for her dead master.

## Mentioned but never seen

- **Silver's wife** — Keeps the Spy-glass inn in Silver's absence; Jim's closing line calls her "his old Negress."
- **The crew of the Walrus** — Flint's dead pirates, invoked in sea-songs and oaths.

## Unnamed figures

Pew's two unnamed companions on the lane; the revenue officers riding with Dance; the "tall man" at the inn door; the Bristol seamen Silver recruits. They appear, threaten, and vanish without names — as is proper.
