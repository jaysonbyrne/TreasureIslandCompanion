ACCOUNT of the DIVISION of the TREASURE

landed ex the Schooner HISPANIOLA at Bristol, & divided
among the Adventurers, viz. —

    To John Trelawney, Esq., Squire .........  one fifth share
    To David Livesey, Doctor of Physick .....  one fifth share
    To Alexander Smollett, late Captain .....  one fifth share
    To Abraham Gray, Seaman .................  one fifth share
    To Jim Hawkins ..........................  one fifth share

    To Ben Gunn .............................  £1,000 in money,
        paid down, & by him spent or lost in nineteen days,
        for which a lodge is settled upon him in lieu thereof.

Deducted & allow'd: one sack of coin, value three to four
hundred guineas, taken & carried off by John Silver, late
sea-cook, at Spanish America; the said Silver being gone
clean out of our knowledge.

The bar silver & the arms lie still where Flint buried
them, & shall lie there for us.

All of us have had an ample share, & used it wisely or
foolishly, according to our natures.

Settled at Bristol, & sign'd —

    John Trelawney
    David Livesey
    Alex'r Smollett
