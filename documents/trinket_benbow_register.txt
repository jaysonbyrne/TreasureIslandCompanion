ADMIRAL BENBOW — Black Hill Cove
Register of Guests & Travellers

March  9th.  Revd. Mr. Chadwick, of St. Anne's — 1 night. Pd.
March 14th.  Thos. Redfern, farmer — 2 nights. Pd.
March 17th.  BILL — [no surname given] — seafaring man.
              Paid one month in advance. Since then, nothing.
April  2nd.  Two drapers from Exeter — 1 night. Pd.
April 21st.  A tinker, name unknown — turned away.
May    6th.  BILL — still here. Owes for lodging, rum, & tobacco.
              Promises payment "when my chest is opened."
June  11th.  BILL — still here. Eats little. Drinks much.
              Will not be ask'd his business.

[In the landlord's neat hand, in the margin:]

The man will give no name but Bill, and no account of
himself. He keeps a brass telescope by him, and a cutlass
under his coat, and starts at every knock. My wife is
afeard of him, and I am not easy myself; yet his money,
when he had money, was good. I shall not press him for
the reckoning till his shipmate comes, as he says one will.
