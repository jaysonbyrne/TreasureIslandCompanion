COME BUY! COME BUY!

The True and Lamentable Ditty of the Pirates,
as sung by FLINT'S OWN CREW (late of the Walrus,
and drown'd or hang'd every man of 'em),
in every tavern from hence to Tortuga.

————————————————————————

          FIFTEEN MEN
             on the
        DEAD MAN'S CHEST

   Fifteen men on the dead man's chest—
      Yo-ho-ho, and a bottle of rum!
   Drink and the devil had done for the rest—
      Yo-ho-ho, and a bottle of rum!

————————————————————————

Learn it, sing it, and take warning by it,
ye jolly tars of Bristol! — for drink and the devil
are still doing for the rest, as many a poor fellow
could tell you if he wasn't at the bottom of the sea.

Printed for the Hawker, and sold for ONE PENNY.
God save the King.
