               THE ADMIRAL BENBOW, Black Hill Cove.

     Reckoning of the Captain, who calls himself BILL.

     To lodging, 14 weeks, at 3s. 6d. the week . . . . .  £2  9s.  0d.
     To rum, 63 glasses, at 2d. . . . . . . . . . . . . .    10s.  6d.
     To bacon and eggs, sundry mornings  . . . . . . . . .    12s.  4d.
     To a chair broke in the parlour (cutlass) . . . . . .     4s.  0d.
                                                          ------------
                                                            £3 15s. 10d.

     Rec'd on acct., 4 gold pieces, being guineas . . . .  £4  4s.  0d.

     Balance in the Captain's favour  . . . . . . . . . .     8s.  2d.

     [chalked beneath, in a large hand]

     HE OWES NOTHING.  HE HAS PAID.  LET HIM LIE.

     [and under that, in ink, in the landlord's hand]

     The above settled this night by my wife out of the
     Captain's own bag, the Captain being dead of apoplexy.
     Not a farthing over our dues.  -- M.H.
