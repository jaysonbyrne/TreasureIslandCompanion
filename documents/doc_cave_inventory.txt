Acount of the Tresure as found in my cave, sett down by me
Ben Gunn, in my own shaky hand, that no man may say me nay.

    Barrs of gold, bild square like a wall ....  63 barrs
    Dubloons, Spanyish ......................  2 bags full
    Ginneas & dubble ginneas, English ........  3 bags full
    Moydoors, Portugee .......................  1 bag & over
    Sequins ..................................  1 small bag
    Lewies, French ...........................  1 bag full
    Peeces of eight, Spanyish ................  4 bags full
    Round peeces & square peeces, & peeces bored
      through the middle, kings of Europe for
      a hunderd year ........................  more'n I can count

Carry'd on my back, many weary jorneys, from the foot of the
tall pine to my cave on the two-pinted hill, two months afore
the Hispanola come. Every bag lick'd & tyed by me, Ben Gunn,
I am.

N.B. — I am not to be robb'd of my cheeze money, which the
Doctor promis'd me, & I will have it toasted.

                                          Ben Gunn
                                          his mark  X
