     [From the account-book found in Billy Bones's chest.
      The first pages bear idle scrawls, as a man with a
      pen might make for idleness or practice:]

     Billy Bones his fancy
     Mr. W. Bones, mate
     No more rum
     Off Palm Key he got itt

     [Then ten or twelve pages of entries: a date at one
      end of the line, a sum of money at the other, and
      between them a varying number of crosses, as in
      common account-books:]

     12 June 1745  . . . . . .  xxxxxx  . . . . . .  £70
      3 Sept 1749  . . . . . .   xxx    . . . . . .  £210
     19 Mar  1752  . . . Offe Caraccas . xxxxxxxx   £480
      7 Nov  1758  . . 62o 17' 20", 19o 2' 40" . .  £1,100
     22 Aug  1763  . . . . . .  xxxxxxxxxxxx  . . . £2,400

     [The record runs near twenty years, the sums growing
      larger as they go.  At the end, after five or six
      wrong additions, a grand total, and these words:]

                         Bones, his pile.

     [In the blank leaves at the end: a few bearings of
      places, and a table for reducing French, English,
      and Spanish moneys to a common value.]
