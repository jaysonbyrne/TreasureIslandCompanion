ARTICLES OF PAROLE

made & agreed this morning at the Block House, between

    JOHN SILVER, late Sea-cook, now Captain by election
of the one part, and

    JIM HAWKINS, Cabin-boy
of the other part.

I.  That the said Jim Hawkins gives his word of honour as
a young gentleman — for a young gentleman he is, although
poor born — that he will not slip his cable, nor attempt
escape, nor hold converse with the enemy through the
palisade, save by leave.

II. That the said John Silver undertakes to keep the said
Jim Hawkins safe from harm at the hands of his shipmates,
to treat him as a guest & hostage, & to bring him down
to the parley with Dr. Livesey unhurt.

III. That bygones are bygones between them, & each stands
by the other, back to back, till this business is ended.

In witness whereof we set our hands.

    Jim Hawkins
    John Silver, his mark X

Witness: Captain Flint, parrot, who squawk'd his assent —
"Pieces of eight!"
