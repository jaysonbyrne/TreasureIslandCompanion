        From the BRISTOL GAZETTE, August, 17—.

The schooner HISPANIOLA, Capt. Smollett, is return'd
from a trading voyage to the Spanish Main, having
landed a considerable quantity of specie and bullion,
the property of the owners. The vessel is much spoken
of for her sailing qualities, and the Captain for his
seamanship in bringing her home short-handed.

We are sorry to learn that of the company that sail'd
from this port, but five men return with her, the rest
having been lost to fever and the hardships of the
climate. The owners desire it to be known that the
families of the deceased will be handsomely provided
for, and that no blame attaches to any person concern'd
in the fitting out of the expedition.

Capt. Smollett speaks in the highest terms of Mr. John
Trelawney, of Trelawney, and of Dr. Livesey, whose
care, he says, preserv'd the survivors through great
perils. The ship's cook, one Silver, is not among those
return'd, having left the vessel at a port in Spanish
America for the recovery of his health.
