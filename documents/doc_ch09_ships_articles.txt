     ARTICLES of Agreement, made and concluded this first
     day of March, in the year of grace 17--, between
     JOHN TRELAWNEY, owner, and ALEXANDER SMOLLETT, master,
     of the good Schooner HISPANIOLA of Bristol, of two
     hundred tons burthen, and the several persons whose
     names or marks are hereunto set, shipped for a voyage
     from the port of Bristol to parts unknown and back.

     I.   Every man shall duly and truly obey the lawful
          commands of the master and officers, on pain of
          forfeiture of wages and such further punishment
          as the law allows.
     II.  No man shall game, quarrel, or come drunk on
          deck when his watch is called, on pain of
          stoppage of his grog.
     III. No arms, powder, or ammunition shall be kept
          private in the forecastle; all to be delivered
          to the master and secured under the cabin.
     IV.  No man shall desert the ship in any port under
          pain of forfeiture of all wages and shares.
     V.   Of all treasure, goods, or prize lawfully taken
          upon this voyage, the division shall be: to the
          owner, one full half; the remainder to be shared
          among master, officers, and company according to
          their stations, as per the scale annexed.
     VI.  He that first sights the island shall have ten
          guineas for his sharpness.
     VII. These articles to be read aloud at the mast
          before sailing, that no man may plead ignorance.

     In witness whereof we set our hands or marks.
