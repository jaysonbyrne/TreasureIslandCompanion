[A page torn from a Bible, about the size of a crown piece —
the last leaf. One side shews a verse or two of the Revelation,
among them these words:]

    ... Without are dogs and murderers ...

[That printed side is blacken'd all over with wood ash, which
comes off & soils the fingers. On the blank side, written in
the same ash, in a fair clerkly hand, the one word:]

    DEPPOSED

[Handled thus: cut out by Dick Johnson with his knife; tipp'd
to John Silver in full council by George Merry's deputation;
toss'd by Silver to Jim Hawkins, who keeps it. Of the writing
now scarce a trace remains beyond a single scratch, such as
a man might make with his thumb-nail.]
