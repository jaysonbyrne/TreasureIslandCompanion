EXTRACT from the LOG of the Schooner HISPANIOLA.

P.M.
1.30 (3 bells) — The two gigs went ashore with the mutineers' party.
The capt., the squire, & myself in council in the cabin. No wind;
nothing to be done with the six rogues left aboard.

2.0 — Hunter & I put off in the jolly-boat for the shore, to learn
news. Discover'd the stockade upon the knoll, with a spring of clear
water — the very thing wanting aboard.

2.30 — Return'd aboard. Settled the plan with the captain. Redruth
posted in the gallery with loaded muskets. Loaded the jolly-boat:
powder-tins, muskets, bags of biscuit, kegs of pork, a cask of
cognac, & my medicine chest.

3.0 — 1st trip ashore. Stores toss'd over the palisade; Joyce left
to guard them. Hunter & I back for a 2nd load.

4.0 — 2nd trip ashore; the whole cargo bestow'd. Hunter & Joyce
left in the block-house. I sculled back to the ship.

5.0 — Loaded for the last time: pork, powder, & biscuit; a musket
& cutlass apiece. Dropp'd the spare arms & powder overboard in
2½ fathoms. Abraham Gray, seaman, join'd the captain's party.

Evening — Abandon'd the HISPANIOLA to the mutineers, & shoved off
for the stockade, seven souls in the jolly-boat.
