                W A N T E D

JOHN SILVER, alias LONG JOHN, late Sea-Cook
of the schooner HISPANIOLA of Bristol.

A tall, stout man, florid of face, wanting the
left leg below the knee, and going upon a crutch.
Speaks fair and smiling, and is the more to be
mistrusted for it. Commonly attended by a large
green Parrot, screaming "Pieces of eight."

The said SILVER deserted the said vessel at a port
in Spanish America, having cut through a bulkhead
and carried off a sack of coin, value three to four
hundred guineas, the property of the owners.

Whoever shall apprehend the said JOHN SILVER, and
lodge him in any of His Majesty's gaols, shall
receive a Reward of FIFTY GUINEAS, paid by the
owners, on his conviction.

No questions ask'd of any person giving information.

D. LIVESEY, Magistrate.
Bristol, August, 17—.

————————————————————————
Caution to all Masters of Vessels: the fellow will
ship under a false name, and talk himself into any
man's good graces inside of a week.
