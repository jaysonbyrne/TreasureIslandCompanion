WATCH BILL for the Block House, by order of John Silver, Cap'n.

The night divided thus, & every man to keep his watch true,
on pain of my displeasure:

    First watch  (8 to 12)   — Tom Morgan
    Middle watch (12 to 4)   — George Merry
    Morn'g watch (4 to 8)    — Dick Johnson

The wounded man excus'd, by the Doctor's order.
The Cap'n walks the rounds himself, & no man sleeps but at
his post.

No man quits the house without leave. The boy Hawkins to be
kept in sight at all hours. Any man found at the brandy
cask out of his turn answers to me.

Set this night at the Block House.

                                        John Silver, Cap'n
                                        his mark  X
