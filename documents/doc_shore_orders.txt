ORDERS FOR SHORING. — J.S.

1st pty. with me — search to the nor'ard for the stockade & the spring,
as per the chart.

2nd pty. — along shore to the east'ard, look for fresh water & a
good landing.

The two gigs to lay off where the river runs in. A man in each boat,
& no man to leave his boat on no account. Whistle Lillibullero &
keep a bright look-out.

No man to fire his piece till I give the word by whistle — three
blasts together. Tom & Alan to keep with the 1st pty.

Any man that desarts his pty., or gives the show away ashore,
let him answer to me for it.

J. Silver
