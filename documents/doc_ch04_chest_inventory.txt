     Inventory of the sea-chest, taken this night at the
     Admiral Benbow by me, JIM HAWKINS, my mother holding
     the candle.

     1 suit of very good clothes, brushed and folded, never worn.
     1 quadrant, in a wooden case.
     1 tin canikin.
     Tobacco, several sticks.
     2 brace of very handsome pistols, loaded.
     1 piece of bar silver.
     1 old Spanish watch, foreign make.
     Sundry trinkets of little value, mostly foreign.
     1 pair of compasses, mounted with brass.
     5 or 6 curious West Indian shells.
     1 old boat-cloak, whitened with sea-salt.
     1 bundle tied up in oilcloth, looking like papers.
     1 canvas bag, that jingles with gold: doubloons, and
       louis d'ors, and guineas, and pieces of eight, and
       I know not what besides, all shaken together.

     The chest itself branded with a B, burned with a hot
     iron, the corners smashed as by long rough usage.

                                        J.H.
