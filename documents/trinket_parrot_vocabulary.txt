Phrases of CAPTAIN FLINT, the Parrot,
belonging to Mr. Silver, Sea-Cook.
Set down by J. Hawkins, cabin-boy, at sea.

"Pieces of eight! Pieces of eight! Pieces of eight!"
   — said very fast, over and over, till you wonder
     the bird is not out of breath.

"Stand by to go about"
   — scream'd whenever the helm is put over, or when
     the bird is startled, or for no reason at all.

A shrill whistle, like a boatswain's pipe, before
any of the above.

Divers oaths, sea-curses, and fore-castle expressions
too coarse to be set down on this page. The Squire
heard two of them at the Spy-glass and turn'd quite
red, and the Doctor laugh'd till the tears stood in
his eyes, and said the bird had plainly sail'd with
gentlemen of the old school.

N.B. — The bird is two hundred years old, by Mr.
Silver's account, and knows more wickedness than
all the crew together. I believe him.
