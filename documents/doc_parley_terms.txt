ARTICLES OF PARLEY, offer'd under a Flag of Truce
by John Silver, Cap'n.

You give us the chart to get the treasure by, & drop shooting
poor seamen. You do that, & we'll offer you a ch'ice.

Either you come aboard along of us, once the treasure's shipp'd,
& then I'll give you my affy-davy, upon my word of honour, to
clap you somewhere safe ashore where you'll be spoke by honest
folk.

Or if that ain't to your fancy — some of my hands being rough
& havin' old scores on account of hazin' — then you can stay here,
you can. We'll divide stores with you, man for man; & I'll give
my affy-davy, as before, to speak the first ship I sight, & send
'em here to pick you up.

Now you'll own that's talkin'. Handsomer you couldn't look to get.
What is spoke to one is spoke to all.

Refuse that, & you've seen the last of me but musket-balls.

John Silver, Cap'n — by my affy-davy.
