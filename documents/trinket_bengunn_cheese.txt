Taken at the stockade, from the relation of
BEN GUNN, mariner, maroon'd three years upon
the island. Set down by D. Livesey.

Being ask'd what had sustain'd him, and what he
most long'd for in his solitude, he answer'd as
follows —

"Goats, and berries, and oysters. Wherever a man
is, says I, a man can do for himself. But, mate,
my heart is sore for Christian diet."

Being press'd further, he grew very earnest, and
said that many's the long night he had dreamed of
cheese — toasted, mostly — and woke up again, and
there he were, with the taste of it gone and the
goats staring at him.

He describ'd the cheese of his dreams at length:
the rind brown'd at the fire, the inside soft and
running, the smell of it filling a whole house.
He said he would give all the doubloons in Flint's
cave for a single toasted cheese, and meant it
from his heart.

The boy hath promis'd him cheese by the stone,
and I have seldom seen a promise give so much
comfort. I have order'd a whole cheese to be set
aside from the stores, and entered it in the
manifest as medicines.

D.L.
