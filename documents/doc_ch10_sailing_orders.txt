     [Sealed with a wafer, and marked: To be opened at sea.]

     SAILING ORDERS.

     To Captain ALEXANDER SMOLLETT, master of the schooner
     HISPANIOLA, now lying in the port of Bristol.

     SIR,--You are hereby required to take command of the
     said schooner, and, the powder and arms being shifted
     to the place under the cabin, to proceed to sea with
     the morning tide.

     Your course and destination are communicated to you
     under separate seal, and to no other soul on board.
     You will keep the ship's reckoning private, and suffer
     no officer to be contradicted in the execution of his
     duty.

     You will maintain strict discipline: watches to be
     kept, lights out at eight bells, no grog served but
     by your order, and the men to be kept from drink
     before the mast.

     The gentlemen's servants are to be berthed beside the
     cabin, and no man of the crew is to come abaft the
     mainmast without orders.

     Given under my hand, at Bristol, this day of March,
     17--.

                                        JOHN TRELAWNEY, Owner.
