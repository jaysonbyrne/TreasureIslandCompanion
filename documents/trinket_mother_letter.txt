Admiral Benbow, Black Hill Cove
My dear Jim,

Your letter come safe by the carrier, and I have read
it till I know it by heart. That you are well, and in
the Squire's good graces, and bound for Bristol to see
the ship, is the best news this house has had since —
well, since before your poor father's illness, and you
know what I mean.

Now mind what I say, and don't laugh at your mother.
Eat properly, and not all salt pork. Keep your coat dry
and your feet drier. Mind the Squire in his humours,
for he is hasty but his heart is sound, and the Doctor
most of all, for he is the best man in England. Write
to me the moment you are aboard, and again from every
port, or I shall lie awake of nights imagining storms.

Your father would have been that proud of you, Jim, to
see you going out into the world like a man. Be worthy
of him, and come home safe, and you will find the inn
kept clean and the sign fresh painted against your
return.

Your loving mother,
M. HAWKINS

P.S. — I have put up your thick stockings and the
plaster for blisters. Do not give them to any sailor.
