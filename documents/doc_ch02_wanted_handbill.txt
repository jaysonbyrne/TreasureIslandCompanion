                              WANTED.

     A SEAFARING MAN, pale and tallowy, wanting TWO FINGERS
     of the LEFT HAND, black hair, low voice, quick step.

     Last seen at the ADMIRAL BENBOW, Black Hill Cove, where
     he quarrelled with the Captain in the parlour and fled
     upon the blind man's coming.

     Believed to be one of Flint's old crew, and privy to
     the business of the black spot.

     A REWARD of TWENTY GUINEAS will be paid for intelligence
     leading to his apprehension, on application to
     Squire TRELAWNEY, or to Dr. LIVESEY, Magistrate.

     Let no innkeeper harbour him.  He drinks, and does not pay.

                                        Bristol, March, 17--.
