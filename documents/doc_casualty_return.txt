RETURN of Killed & Wounded in the Defence of the Stockade.
— D. Livesey, Surgeon.

KILL'D.
Thos. Redruth, gamekeeper — kill'd in the first day's fighting.
Buried in the sand by the stockade.
Mr. Joyce, servant — shot through the head in the attack; never
mov'd again.

WOUNDED.
Mr. Hunter, servant — stunn'd by a blow from a musket wrench'd
through his loophole; senseless some hours; like to do well.
Capt. Alex. Smollett — wounded in the attack; under my care.
The squire & Mr. Gray & the boy Hawkins, by God's mercy, unhurt.

OF THE ENEMY, five fell & will never run again: four within the
palisade & one without. Among them Job Anderson, the boatswain,
cut down by Gray; one shot at a loophole in the act of firing;
one dispos'd of by myself. The rest fled to the woods.

By my count the mutineers, nineteen at the outset & fifteen before
the attack, are now not above ten effective men — two of those
wounded, & one, the man shot beside the gun, like to die of it.

D.L.
