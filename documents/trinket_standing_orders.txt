STANDING ORDERS
for the Ship's Company of the Schooner HISPANIOLA.
By command of ALEXR. SMOLLETT, Master.
To be nail'd to the Forecastle, and read aloud
every Sunday after Prayers.

I. The Ship's Company shall keep two Watches, Starboard
and Larboard, reliev'd at four Hours, and no Man shall
sleep upon his Watch on pain of stoppage of Grog.

II. Grog shall be serv'd at Noon, half a Pint to a Man,
and stopp'd for Drunkenness, Quarrelling, or Neglect
of Duty.

III. No lighted Pipe nor Candle below Decks after Eight
Bells of the Evening Watch. No Spirits to be brought
aboard save by the Captain's Warrant.

IV. Arms shall be exercis'd every Sunday Morning, fair
Weather permitting, all Hands to attend, and no Man
to quit the Deck without Leave of the Officer of
the Watch.

V. No Officer of this Ship shall be contradicted in the
Execution of his Duty, on pain of such Punishment as
the Articles provide.

VI. Cleanliness is next to Godliness. Hammocks to be
scrubb'd the first Saturday of every Month.

God save the King.
A. SMOLLETT.
