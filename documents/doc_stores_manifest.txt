MANIFEST of Stores landed & secur'd in the Block-House.

Powder, in tins ......................... 8
Muskets, with bayonets ................... 20
Cutlasses ............................... 7
Cartridges, made up ...................... 60
Biscuit, in bags ......................... 6
Pork, in kegs ............................ 4
Cognac, in cask .......................... 1
Medicine chest (Dr. Livesey's) ........... 1
Spare flints, powder-horns, &c. .......... as per list

LOSSES. — The jolly-boat, overladen on her last trip, sank by the
stern in 3 feet of water off the landing. A good half of the powder
& provisions lost with her; 3 muskets drown'd & unserviceable; only
2 guns out of 5 sav'd in a state for service from that trip.

The stores above are all that stand between seven souls & starvation.
Expend with the extremest care.

Examin'd.  Alex. Smollett, Capt.
