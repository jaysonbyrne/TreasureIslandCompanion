Loopholes: North side, five (Mr. Trelawney & Gray).
South side (porch), two. East & west, two each.
Firewood pil'd in four heaps, one to each side; ammunition &
four loaded muskets laid ready to hand at each. Cutlasses rang'd
in the middle.

SURVEYOR'S NOTE. — The place commands the spring, which is the
key of the island; the clearing gives a fair field of fire on
every side; the paling is too strong to pull down without time
& labour, & too open to shelter besiegers. Short of a complete
surprise, seven resolute men may hold it against a regiment.
Defects: the wood stands too close for defence on the land side;
sand in everything; the chimney but a square hole in the roof,
so the smoke near blinds us. A good watch & strict economy of
stores are all we want.

A.S.
