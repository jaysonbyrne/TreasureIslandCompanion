     PRECAUTIONS agreed this day in the cabin of the
     HISPANIOLA, the island being in sight.

     I.   The powder and all firearms to remain secured
          under the cabin, and none to be served out but
          by the captain's own hand.
     II.  Watches to be doubled henceforth, day and night,
          and no boat to leave the ship without orders.
     III. Mr. Trelawney's three servants to be berthed
          beside the cabin, and to keep their arms loaded.
     IV.  The gentlemen to go armed at all times, and the
          boy Hawkins to keep with Dr. Livesey or the
          captain.
     V.   A bright lookout to be kept for the first sign
          of treachery among the crew; the men are not to
          know themselves suspected.

     We are seven against nineteen, counting the boy, and
     must trust to watchfulness till we know our men.

     Agreed and signed,

          ALEXR. SMOLLETT, Master.
          JOHN TRELAWNEY.
          DAVID LIVESEY.
